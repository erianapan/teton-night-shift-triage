Where the data comes from, and where it goes

One scenario, traced through Teton's hardware and surfaces: a possible fall in room 05 at 03:10, and the nurse who reaches it three minutes later.

The loop closes inside the room. The nurse walking in at 03:13 is not a confirmation she taps, it is sensor input the building already collects, so the alert can be marked attended with nothing pressed.

The two assumptions this drawing makes

Named staff The sensor detects a person, not a named person. Identity most likely comes from which phone is signed in on shift, matched to the room the sensors see occupied. Where identity is unknown the screen should still say "a colleague is in room 07", which is the fact that matters.
Attended, not resolved A person entering the room means somebody is there. It does not mean the resident is fine, and the person could be a relative. Presence marks an alert attended. Closing it still needs a human.
